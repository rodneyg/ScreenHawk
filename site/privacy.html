Privacy policy

Rodney Gainous Jr publishes ScreenHawk. ScreenHawk has no account, developer server, or analytics. This website uses no analytics or cookies.

On your device

ScreenHawk stores your own OpenAI API key and model choice on this device in chrome.storage.local. The default model is gpt-4.1-nano. When you start a capture, Chrome captures the visible tab. ScreenHawk crops the image to the area you select on your device.

What OpenAI receives

When you submit, ScreenHawk sends only the selected crop and your typed question as content directly to api.openai.com. The request includes your chosen model and uses your own API key for authorization. The answer appears only on the page where you started the capture. OpenAI’s privacy policy applies to processing by OpenAI.

Rodney Gainous Jr does not receive, sell, or share your key, screenshots, or questions.

Chrome permissions

  • activeTab gives ScreenHawk temporary access to the tab where you start a capture.
  • scripting loads the selection, question, and answer interface on demand after you start a capture.
  • storage saves your OpenAI API key and model choice on this device.
  • https://api.openai.com/* lets ScreenHawk send your submitted crop and question directly to OpenAI.

Remove your data

Remove your key in ScreenHawk’s options or uninstall the extension to remove its local storage. You can also revoke the key from your OpenAI account. Contact OpenAI about any data it has already processed.
