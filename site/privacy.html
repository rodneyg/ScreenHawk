Privacy policy

Rodney Gainous Jr publishes ScreenHawk. ScreenHawk has no account, developer server, or analytics. This website uses no analytics or cookies.

On your device

ScreenHawk stores your OpenAI API key only on this device in chrome.storage.local. Your browser captures the visible tab and crops the image to the area you select.

What OpenAI receives

When you submit a question, your browser sends the selected screenshot area and your question directly to api.openai.com using your own API key. The response appears on your tab. OpenAI’s privacy policy applies to processing by OpenAI.

Rodney Gainous Jr does not receive, sell, or share your key, screenshots, or questions.

Chrome permissions

  • activeTab gives ScreenHawk temporary access to the tab where you start a capture.
  • scripting lets ScreenHawk open the selection and question interface on a tab.
  • tabs lets ScreenHawk find the active tab for capture and show the answer there.
  • storage saves your OpenAI API key locally.
  • <all_urls> lets the selection interface run on regular web pages and allows requests to OpenAI.

Remove your data

Remove your key in ScreenHawk’s options or uninstall the extension to remove its local storage. You can also revoke the key from your OpenAI account. Contact OpenAI about any data it has already processed.
